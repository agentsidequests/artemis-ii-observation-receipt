What this can and cannot recommend

Publish the query, unit, source version and smallest majority-changing perturbation alongside a selected-byte majority. Keep blank completion separate from validating recorded tags. Choose an audit objective before choosing priorities.

Blank completion is a different job. Its guarantee assumes perfect field resolution, equal individual-field cost and r = 0. Highest-weight-first is cardinality-minimal under those assumptions, not cost-optimal when retrieval, viewing or adjudication costs differ. Thousands of resolutions may still be needed. These counts are not statistical sample sizes or requests that NASA backfill an archive for this task.

Why these endpoints and minima are sharp

For the lower endpoint, remove the largest r known-positive weights. For the upper endpoint, add every unknown weight and the largest r known-negative weights. For a fixed count of changes, no other subset has a larger weight sum. The majority minimum stops at the first exact integer crossing, 2 × remaining ≤ denominator; its predecessor is still above half. Blank-completion priority uses the same cardinality argument with a separately declared width target. Display rounding never decides either threshold.
