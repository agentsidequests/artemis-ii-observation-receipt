# Product contract

## Observable outcome

A researcher can run one command against the included Artemis II case, open a local viewer, and distinguish a supported event ledger from a refuted coverage claim, a capability-blocked camera null, a supported count floor, a measurement-blocked flux claim, and a witness-dependence block.

The separate 0.3 identity demo adds unresolved source-linked alternatives and an original/conditional metric comparison. Its exact invariants and bounded acceptance are in `IDENTITY-COMPARISON-CONTRACT.md`. It preserves the original flash case rather than turning camera-action uncertainty into an altered event ledger.

The separate target-weight case has its own case-version 1 contract and schema in `ROBUSTNESS-CONTRACT.md` and `schemas/tag-weight-case-v1.schema.json`. Enter it with `demo --case robustness`. It consumes a frozen accounting analysis with hypothetical assumption controls and exact source-row witnesses, not physical label validation or a new generic truth-engine rule. Existing 0.2 and 0.3 cases keep their versions and outputs.

## First user and entry surface

- User: researcher, citizen-science coordinator, data journalist, or technical reviewer.
- Goal: determine whether available evidence had the opportunity and capability to test a stated claim.
- Entry: repository `README.md`, then `python run.py demo`.
- Context: local computer, Python 3.11 or newer, no account or network.

## Governing invariant

The app must preserve the difference among not marked, not exposed, exposed without demonstrated capability, observed and rejected, and supported. It must not turn missing evidence into a negative observation.

## Falsifier

The approach fails if a first-time user cannot tell why the later camera window and overlapping camera window produce different blocked or refuted outcomes, or if a mutation to time, capability, artifact identity, measurement fields, or witness dependence does not change the relevant result.

## Recovery path

Invalid case structure exits with an actionable validation error and no receipt. Artifact mismatch produces a failed receipt that identifies the affected artifact. No source evidence is modified.

## Acceptance boundary

Automated checks and an author walkthrough can establish structural and live-operator behavior only. Uncoached first-use comprehension, scientific acceptance, cross-domain value, accessibility for disabled users, and maintained usefulness remain unverified until observed separately.
