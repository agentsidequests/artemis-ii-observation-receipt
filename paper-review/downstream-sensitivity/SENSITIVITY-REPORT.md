# Artemis II: withholding one ambiguous original sequence

Private conditional sensitivity report, 12 October 2026

## Result

All eight named conclusion tests retain their original Boolean disposition when the affected original five-frame sequence is withheld. Several quantities change, so this is not numerical identity and not a replacement census. It is evidence that these specific conclusions do not depend on admitting that one sequence under the declared calculation.

The relationship funnel becomes **4,509 = 4,305 + 204 + 0**. Sunset remains a minority of selected actions and a majority of combined selected archive bytes. The target-field completion interval remains wide enough to span both sides of 50%. The detection receipts are preserved, not reanalyzed.

This does not validate physical identity, all reconstruction choices, target truth, a real model's behavior, scientific acceptance, or NASA's pipeline. The original receipts and source bytes remain unchanged.

Every surviving manifest field, split assignment, action key, temporal-cluster key and schedule interval is held fixed. In particular, temporal clusters are not rebuilt after withholding. The alternative `art002e009301` and the eight additional withheld candidate families are not added. This is a leave-one-original-action-out sensitivity, not adoption of a new reconstruction, rerandomized evaluation or physical deduplication.

For window weights `w`, the concentration index is `(sum w)^2 / sum(w^2)`. Equal weights would yield the number of windows; concentrated weights yield a smaller index. Raw memberships can carry an action into overlapping windows more than once, which is why fractional allocation and same-name-collapse sensitivities are kept separate. None makes a scheduled window a validated independent event.

## Why the completion result stays uncertain

Let `N` be source capture keys, `K` recorded Sunset memberships and `U` blank target fields. Keeping every recorded nonblank field fixed, the possible completed Sunset field share lies between `K/N` and `(K+U)/N`.

Originally `(N, K, U) = (7,606, 1,553, 4,193)`; conditionally it is `(7,601, 1,553, 4,193)`. No blanks are resolved by removing five recorded Moon fields. The strict-majority tipping point is `floor(N/2) + 1 - K`, giving 2,251 and 2,248 additional blank memberships.

The worst-case individual-field resolution budgets remain 3,433, 3,813 and 4,117 to make the completion width at most 10, five and one percentage points. These are deterministic record-completion budgets, not sample-size advice. A content-label audit must also assess recorded values; metadata completion alone cannot prove image semantics.
