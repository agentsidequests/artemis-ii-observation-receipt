# Artemis II interactive science flagship, v10

One local Explore / Play / Use / Inspect experience. Explore retains the lunar visual story; Play adds three untimed experiments; Use opens the four existing apps and downloads; Inspect retains the exact full notebook, sources, math, limitations and separate proposed pilots. [Guide](presentation/flagship/GUIDE.md).

The train/test miniature is invented and trains no model. Its task is a new camera sequence, not a universal boundary or a validated new-event test. The recorded-field completion range comes from existing assumption-bound arithmetic, not image truth, likelihood or a confidence interval. The recorder is wholly invented, with arbitrary signal units and a perfect deterministic rule; none of its controls maps to the actual uncalibrated Artemis clip test.

The original filename/order-conditioned grouping, complete scientific inputs, four apps, v6 essay/charts, v9 source/output and full technical notebook stay unchanged. JPL confirmed one pair only, with no subtraction from the original cohort. All existing clock, calibration, photometry, identity, dependence, location and flux limits remain active. NASA imagery retains its credits and terms; own new code and analysis remain MIT-licensed, credited agentsidequests.

Source/build/model/package checks and selected real-browser operator paths cannot establish all-age comprehension, enjoyment, complete accessibility, learning efficacy, owner taste or scientific acceptance. This is a local prototype, not a public release, hosted service, NASA endorsement or new archive standard. No upload, remote push, contact or publication occurs.
