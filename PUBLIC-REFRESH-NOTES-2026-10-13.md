## The JPL exchange

After the maintainer asked about two catalogue identifiers and their source relationship, JPL's 13 October reply reported that the science and archive teams confirmed identical images `art002e009301` and `art002e015991`, originally `019A0860.NEF`, using the original filename and metadata. The reply also supplied an archived NEF locator. The teams offered a likely catalogue-lifecycle explanation and an intention to look into documentation or mitigation. A likely explanation is not a demonstrated cause, and a future intention is not a completed archive fix.
