## Safe synthesis

The claims combine into two task-conditioned gates:

`archive representations × exposure protocol × observation grouping × chosen estimand → apparent scale and statistical influence`

The representation multiplier creates absolute file scale. Variable exposure protocol creates differential action weight. Group and event reconstruction determine which records are related. The task's estimand determines whether product, exposure, action, realized segment, planned event, or byte is the relevant outer unit.

For an evaluation claim, the protected relation determines whether a test record represents a genuinely unseen opportunity at the level the claim requires. For an observational null or rate claim, demonstrated coverage and recovery determine whether a frame, observer, or sensor contributes a genuine opportunity to detect the phenomenon.

The most compact safe interpretation separates four axes:

- **Independence:** graph edges determine which products, captures, actions, segments, and planned events are related.
- **Influence:** multiplicities plus the chosen outer estimand determine how much weight each related observation receives.
- **Burden:** bytes determine storage, transport, and processing load, not observational independence or scientific importance.
- **Detectability:** live time, field of view, response, threshold, and recovery determine whether an observation channel can support a null or physical rate claim.

For any admitted set of complete actions, the accounting telescope is exact:

`bytes = actions × (exposures / actions) × (products / exposures) × (bytes / products)`

Stage 11I shows that `products / exposures` is constant at three in the admitted cohort, while `exposures / actions` varies with protocol. Stage 11K shows that `bytes / products` adds a separate file-size layer. Stage 11R shows that all 4,511 canary test files can be structurally linked back to training without proving leakage or harm. Stage 11Q shows the mirror image: 900 frames can fail to produce an exclusionary null when the screen has poor demonstrated recovery. The graph, estimand, and measurement function, not any one scalar total, supply the missing interpretation.

## Global ceiling

The ledger supports a methodological and open-science analysis. It does not support a new lunar-science discovery, calibrated photometry, formal informative cluster size, observed model leakage or bias, an archive defect, global novelty, or priority.
