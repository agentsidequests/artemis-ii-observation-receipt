Jack Schmitt/Apollo 17 observation of lunar impact
"NASA Apollo 17 transcript" discussion is given below (before descent to lunar surface):
---------------------
03 15 38 09 (mission elapsed time)
 (10 Dec 1972, 21:16:09 UT – possible Geminid)
LMP Hey, I just saw a flash on the lunar surface!
CC Oh, yes?
LMP It was just out there north of Grimaldi [mare]. Just north of Grimaldi. You might see if 
you got anything on your seismometers, although a small impact probably would give a fair 
amount of visible light.
CC Okay. We'll check.
LMP It was a bright little flash right out there near that crater. See the [sharp rimed] crater 
right at the [north] edge of [the] Grimaldi [mare]? Then there is another one [i.e., sharp 
rimed crater] [directly] north of it [about 50km]- fairly sharp one north of it. [That] is where 
there was just a thin streak [pin prick] [flash?] of light.
CC How about putting an X on the map where you saw it?
LMP I keep looking for -- yes, we will. I was planning on looking for those kind of things....
 Geminids 12/13/1972
x
Jack Schmitt - NASA

vsma.nasa.gov
▪ Field of view encompasses approximately 
4 million square kilometers of lunar surface
▪ Resolution of ~4 km 
▪ Complete to magnitude +9 – corresponds 
to a mass of about 30 g at sporadic speed 
of 20 km s-1 (ping pong ball size)
▪ LunarScan software (Pete Gural) used to 
detect impact flashes – require 
simultaneous detections in both telescopes
▪ ArcGIS software used to selenolocate 
impact flashes – refraction correction also 
applied
