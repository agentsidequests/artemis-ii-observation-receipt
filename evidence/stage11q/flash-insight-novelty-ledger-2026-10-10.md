## Ranked public insights

1. **The numerator is credible; the denominator is missing.** Five is the start of a measurement, not yet a calibrated impact rate.
2. **The camera "null" splits into two different records.** One is later and cannot test the accepted events. The overlapping one has an empirically insensitive screen.
3. **Five is broadly plausible, not automatically mysterious.** Modern survey extrapolation and Orion's close, dark vantage make the count unsurprising in order, while still scientifically valuable.
4. **The human observers were part of the instrument.** Future releases need observer exposure, prompt state, and per-channel detection functions, not only camera specifications.
5. **A useful null needs a receipt.** Coverage, capability, calibration, and completeness must accompany the word "not detected."

## Recommended narrative limit

The public analysis may say that the Testbed found a timing mismatch, recovered a contextual overlap clip, measured its threshold-defined footprint, and showed that the frozen digital screen failed injection recovery. It should attribute the five impacts, their likely cause, their approximate size, and the sensor recommendations to NASA, and attribute ground-survey rate context to the cited surveys.
